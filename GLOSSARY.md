# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and what that does to a learner's enrollment and an instructor's revenue.

## Language

### Enrollment

**Enrollment**:
A learner's place on a course. While it gives access, the learner can watch the course and their progress counts.

**Active Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that gives access and isn't finished yet.

**Completed Enrollment**:
An Enrollment whose learner has finished the course. It still gives access.
_Avoid_: Ended, Finished (for an Enrollment that lost access)

**Refunded Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that no longer gives access because its Refund Request was approved; its progress, completion and rating are kept but stop counting. A Completed Enrollment can become Refunded, and buying the course again brings access and earlier progress back.
_Avoid_: Ended, Cancelled, Revoked

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request. The fourteenth day counts in full.

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request on a purchase: inside the Refund Window, less than two hours watched, fewer than half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat. It decides who may ask, not what the admin decides — an admin may approve a request even if the learner has since stopped being eligible.
_Avoid_: Refund policy (for the admin's decision)

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an admin has accepted. Approving it starts a Pending Refund and ends the learner's access to the course straight away, before any money moves.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an admin has turned down, with a written reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request if they are still within Refund Eligibility — the Refund Window never restarts.

**Decline Reason**:
The admin's explanation, shown to the learner, of why their Refund Request was declined.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. A learner gets at most one Refund per course, so if they buy the course again after a Refund, that purchase cannot be refunded.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the learner's access stays ended. An admin can try again and later mark it Issued.

### Team licences

**Team Licence**:
One purchase of several seats on a course, handed out to team members as Seat Codes. It can only be refunded as a whole, and only while none of its seats has been claimed.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Group order

**Seat Code**:
A code that gives one team member an Enrollment from a Team Licence. Unclaimed Seat Codes stop working once the licence's Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Coupon (for a team seat)

### Instructor revenue

**Earned Revenue**:
Money from purchases that the instructor can count on: no Refund Request is open on it and no Refund has been approved. The platform fee is taken only from Earned Revenue.
_Avoid_: Revenue (unqualified), Income

**On Hold Revenue**:
Money from a purchase with an Open Refund Request, or with a Pending or Failed Refund. It is neither earned nor gone, and is shown as a figure for right now rather than spread over past months. If the Refund Request is declined, it becomes Earned again.
_Avoid_: Pending (that is a Refund approved but not yet sent), Frozen, Escrow

**Refunded Revenue**:
Money that has left the instructor's revenue because a Refund was Issued. It counts against the month the Refund was Issued, not the month of the purchase, so past months never change.
