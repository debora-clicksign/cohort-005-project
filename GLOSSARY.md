# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around buying courses and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence. Buyers, Team Admins and anyone who claims a Seat are all learners.
_Avoid_: Student, User

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A learner who buys and manages a Team Licence. A Team Admin has no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

**Buyer**:
The learner who paid for a specific Purchase. Only the Buyer can raise a Refund Request on it.
_Avoid_: Customer, Purchaser

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase a learner makes for themselves. It gives the buyer a Place on the course.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not the course itself; the buyer can't watch the course through it.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One share of a Team Licence that a learner can claim to get a Place on the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat no learner has claimed yet.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a learner has claimed. It belongs to that learner, so a Team Licence with any Claimed Seat can't be refunded.

**Place on a course**:
A learner's access to a course, from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat. A learner has at most one per course.
_Avoid_: Enrollment, Access

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once, and none after one is approved.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. The buyer keeps their Place on the course while it is open, and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it ends what the Purchase granted (for an Individual Purchase, the buyer's Place on the course, unless they also hold one through a Claimed Seat; for a Team Licence, its Unclaimed Seats, which can then never be claimed) and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. Nothing the Purchase granted is lost, it has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to the buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request has been approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Refund rules

**Refund Window**:
The whole days after the day of a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: Grace period, Cooling-off period

**Watched**:
Seconds of video a learner has actually played in a course: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

**Watch Limit**:
The amount Watched past which a Refund Request can't be raised on an Individual Purchase, because the learner has had the course.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

**Completion Limit**:
The share of a course's lessons completed past which a Refund Request can't be raised on an Individual Purchase.
_Avoid_: Progress limit
